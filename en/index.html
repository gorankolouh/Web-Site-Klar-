Why a website

Without a professional website, opportunities slip away.

What’s included?

All the terms at a glance – before you decide.

Scope
A single page with the sections we record in the order confirmation, for example introduction, services, about and contact. You supply the text, logo and images; we structure and design them.
Revisions
The number of revision rounds is limited and set in the order confirmation before the project starts. Unlimited revisions are not included. Further changes can be quoted separately.
Hosting
The website is published under the agreed hosting arrangement. Where it is hosted and whether any recurring costs apply is confirmed in writing before you commit.
Domain and renewal
Registration of an available domain for the first year is included. Premium domains and certain extensions may be excluded. We tell you the renewal price from year two in advance.
Ownership and handover
Whose name the domain is registered in, who owns the website and how you can take it over or move it later are set out in the order confirmation.
Third-party costs
If a third party charges fees, for example for form delivery, booking or payment services, we tell you before you order. Nothing is charged without your approval.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website from Klarseite Studio cost?

The starter package is a one-time €99 for the clearly defined scope. Any additional work or third-party costs are always agreed with you in advance – we charge nothing without your approval.

How long does it take?

Our aim is to deliver within 48 hours once all required content, materials and approvals are in, within the agreed scope.

Is the website optimised for smartphones?

Yes. A responsive build for smartphones, tablets and desktops is always included.

Is the domain included in the price?

Registration of an available domain is included for the first year, on the agreed terms. Certain extensions or premium domains may be excluded. We tell you in writing, before you order, whose name the domain is registered in and what renewal costs from year two.

Are hosting and email included too?

Registering a domain does not automatically include hosting or email inboxes. Your website is published under the agreed hosting model, which we explain up front. Email inboxes are not included. We tell you about any recurring costs before you commit.

Can I extend my website later?

Yes. We are happy to quote separately for extra sections, subpages, features or design changes.

Can I add bookings or online payments?

Yes, as an optional add-on with a separate quote. Fees charged by the relevant providers (e.g. booking or payment services) may apply and are stated in advance.

Do you guarantee a top ranking on Google?

No. We implement proven SEO fundamentals, but we cannot guarantee specific rankings or traffic – and nobody honest can.

What information do I need to provide?

Usually: your business name, your services, contact details, your logo (if you have one) and suitable images or other agreed content. For business websites in Germany we also need the details for your legal notice (Impressum).

How does the WhatsApp integration work?

By default, your website includes a WhatsApp button that opens a chat with your business number. Alternatively, the contact form can generate a pre-filled WhatsApp message. We decide together which option to use. As WhatsApp is a third-party service, we mention it in your privacy policy.

What payment methods are available?

We confirm the payment method and timing in the order confirmation before the project begins.

Who owns the finished website?

Ownership, access and handover are set out in the order confirmation or service agreement. So you know in advance exactly what you receive and how you can take over or move your website later.
